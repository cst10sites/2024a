My Lasangna Recipe

Why This Recipe?

Lasagna has been my favourite food all my life. I've spent every second since I started cooking trying to make the best recipe possible. I've tried probably over a dozen recipes and have created a simple, easy, and tasty way to make lasagna. This recipe has minimal ingredients, practical cooking, and maximum enjoyment!

Ingredients

A list of the needed ingredients:

  • 1 pound of beef: This easy lasagna starts with ground beef. Ground turkey could be a substitue.
  • 1 (32 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce.
  • 32 ounces of cheeses: You’ll need cottage cheese, mozzarella, and Parmesan.
  • 2 eggs: Eggs help bind the cheese mixture together. Plus, they lend moisture and richness.
  • 9 lasagna noodles: Of course, you’ll need lasagna noodles!
  • Water: Pour ½ cup of water around the edges of the baking dish before baking.

    • Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees℉ (175 degrees°C)
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook ground beef in the skillet until browned and falling apart, this generally takes 5 to 8 minutes. Discard grease. Add in spaghetti sauce, stir and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Combine cottage cheese, 2 cups of mozzarella cheese, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup of the grated Parmesan cheese, with seasonings of your choice in a large bowl.
  4. Spread 3/4 cup of sauce in a baking dish. Cover with 3 uncooked lasagna noodles, 1 and 3/4 cups of the cheese mixture, and 1/4 cup sauce; repeat layers once more. Top with remaining 3 noodles and ingredients. Pour water along the edges of the dish. Cover with aluminum foil.
  5. Bake in oven for 45 minutes. Take off cover and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Let sit for 10 minutes before serving.
