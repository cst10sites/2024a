My Banana Bread Recipe

Introduction

Banana bread has an interesting history, invented during the great depression. The original recipes for banana "quick bread", created as a use for bananas instead of yeast to make a nice bread. I picked banana bread because it is quick and easy to make yet super delicious.


Ingredients

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 Teaspoon of ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ⅓ tablespoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Heat The oven up to 325°F. (168°C.).
  2. Butter the pan.
  3. Mix the sugar and butter until light and fluffy.
  4. Add one egg at a time. Beat them well after each addition.
  5. Mash the bananas in a small bowl.
  6. Mix the cinnamon and milk into the mashed bananas.
  7. In a different bowl mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  8. Add the Banana mixture to the sugar and butter.
  9. Add the other ingredients and mix until the flour is not visible.
  10. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for an hour to an hour and ¼.
  11. Insert a toothpick and when the pick comes out clean the banana bread is ready.
  12. Leave the banana bread to cool for 15 minutes.
  13. Enjoy!
