How to make an omelette

History

Many people think that omeletes come from France, since the word "omelete" comes from "alumete", which is a French word. Other historians believe that omeletes may come from the Roman empire, because there's evidence that the used to whip and fold eggs. But, the food comes from the Persians, maybe one day they had the idea to fold the egg instead of mixing them.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ cup of milk
  • 1 spoon of butter
  • 1 tea spoons of salt
  • ½ tea spoons of pepper mint
  • Any toppings you want to put(crispy bacon, bell pepper, shredded cheddar, caramelized onions, etc...)

Steps

  1. Break the 2 eggs in a bowl.
  2. Place the milk inside the bowl with the eggs and start whisking (it's important to use side whisking so that the consistency of the eggs is fluffier.
  3. After the milk and the eggs are mixed, add the salt and pepper and whisk again.
  4. Put the stove on medium heat and place the butter on the center of the pan.
  5. After the butter has melted, add the eggs and whisk with a chopstick until the lower part of the egg is solid.
  6. Wait 1 minute till the lower part of the egg seems solid enough to start folding.
  7. Firstly, fold the part that is closer to you until you reach the middle, then continue with the opposite side, and finally flip the egg so the interior isn't raw.
  8. Place in a plate and add the desired toppings.
