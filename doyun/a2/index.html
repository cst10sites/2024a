How to Make Tteokbokki

On this webpage, you will learn how to make delicious Tteokbokki.

Ingredients

Tteokbokki

  • Rice cake: 4 cups (400g)
  • Wheat flour cake: 4 cups (400g)
  • Cabbage: 2 cups (160g)
  • Green onion: 3 cups (240g)
  • Water: 1L

Sauce

  • Red chili paste: ⅓ cup (80g)
  • Dark soy sauce: ⅓ cup (50g)
  • Fine red pepper powder: ⅓ cup (30g)
  • Coarse red pepper powder: ⅓ cup (20g)
  • Brown sugar: ⅓ cup (70g)
  • MSG: ½ tbsp (5g)

Directions

  1. Cut the green onion diagonally or slice in half and cut into long pieces.
  2. Cut the cabbage into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Add water, dark soy sauce, brown sugar, red chili paste, coarse red pepper powder, fine red pepper powder, green onion, and cabbage to a pot and let it boil.
  4. Lightly rinse the wheat flour cake under water.
  5. Once the sauce boils, add rice cake and wheat flour cake, and continue cooking.
  6. Add MSG if desired.
  7. Continue cooking until the sauce reaches the desired consistency.
  8. And it's all done! Enjoy your delicious tteokbokki!
