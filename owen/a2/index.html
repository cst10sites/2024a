How to Make: Poutine

What is Poutine?

Poutine is potato-based dish that originated in Warwick, Quebec in the early 50’s. The dish consists of a French fry base, topped with squeaky cheese curds, and then drenched in an ocean of gravy. Just thinking of this dish makes your mouth water.

Ingredients

  • ½ Beef or chicken gravy
  • 300g of Cheese curds
  • 3 Large russet potatoes
  • Green onions or any other toppings (optional)

Instructions

  1. Start by dicing your russet potatoes into long strips
  2. Once done, evenly disperse through the air fryer. I would recommend 12 to 15 minutes at 350°C
  3. While the fries are cooking, start preparing the gravy by heating up some beef or chicken gravy on the stove.
  4. Once the fries are ready, place them in a serving dish and sprinkle your cheese curds on top
  5. Pour the hot gravy over the fries and curds
  6. Add any toppings you would like (I recommend green onions and bacon if you have access.)
  7. Let it sit for a few minutes and once the gravy won’t burn your mouth, Dig in!
