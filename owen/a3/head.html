<!DOCTYPE html> the <!DOCTYPE html> tag lets your computer know that the file is HTML 5 No Examples
<html lang="en"> the <html lang="en"> lets the browser know that the website is in english or whatever language you choose. the "en" allows the browser to translate my text

<title> the <title> tag is used to display the title in the top tab
<style> This tag describes the internal CSS <style> body {color: blue;}<style>

<link rel="icon"type="image/x-icon" href="favicon.ico"> This tag displays a favicon on your tab for your website.
