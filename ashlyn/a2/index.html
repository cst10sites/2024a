Pumpkin Chocolate Muffin Recipe

Introduction:

In this recipe, we'll be making some scrumptious pumpkin chocolate chip muffins. These muffins have the perfect texture and balance of flavors, with the added bonus of chocolate chips to make them extra tasty. They're a great snack or dessert, and perfect for when you're on the go!

Ingredients:

  • 1 and ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 and ½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup light or dark brown sugar
  • 1 and ½ cups canned pumpkin purée
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, and salt together until combined.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk the oil, granulated sugar, brown sugar, pumpkin puree, eggs, and milk together until combined.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
  5. Add chocolate chips into the mixed batter.
  6. Spoon the batter into liners, filling them all the way to the top.
  7. Bake for 20 minutes at 425°F.
  8. Let rest and then enjoy.
