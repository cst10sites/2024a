How to make "Omurice/オムライス"

Omurice is a Japanese dish consisting of an omelette made with fried rice and thin, fried scrambled eggs, usually topped with ketchup. It is a popular dish also commonly cooked at home.

-Ingredients-

  • onions
  • skinless chiken thigh
  • frozen mixed vegetables
  • Japanese short-grain rice
  • ketchup and soy sauce
  • egg

-Steps for cokking 1-

  1. Mince onion.
  2. Dice 1 boneless skinless chicken thigh into 1/2 inch.
  3. Cook the onions and the chicken.
  4. Add some frozen mixed vegetables and the jepanese short-grain rice.
  5. Add 1 table spoon of ketchup and soy sauce.

↑This is how you make your fried rice!↑

-Steps for cokking 2-

  1. Whisk 1 egg and 1 table spoon milk and mix it.
  2. Haat some oil untl it gets hot.
  3. When the pan is hot, pour in the egg.

↑This is how you make your omlet!↑

-Last step-

  1. Add your fried rice on a plate, then add your omlet on the top
  2. You can add some ketchup, mayonnaise, whenever you like for the toppings.

And this is how you make your Omurice!
