Asian noodle and beef salad

Hungry, and don't feel like spending that much time cooking? Well, this is a quick and tasty meal containing 400 calorie(about 32 minutes of running). Can be pre-made, refrigerated, and served cold. Only takes up to 30 minutes to make, making this a great meal for dinner if youre looking for something new!

Ingredients

  • ¼ reduced sodium soy sauce
  • ¼ cup lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh gingerroot
  • 1 tablespoon seasame oil
  • 1 beef top sirloin steak (1 pound)
  • ¼ tablespoon pepper
  • 6 ounces thin rice noodles
  • 1 cup julienned zucchini
  • 2 medium carrots, thinly sliced
  • 1 celery rib, sliced.

Instructions

  • 1.) To make seasoning, mix the first 6 indgredients top to bottom of the list.
  • 2.)Sprinkle the steak with pepper.
  • 3.)Grill covered, using medium heat until the steak reaches desired doneness.
  • 4.)For medium rare, you could use a thermometer and it should read 135
  • 5.)Take 6-8 minutes per side.
  • 6.)Once done, let it stand out for 5 minutes before slicing into strips
  • 7.) In the meantime, Prepare the rice noodles using package directions.
  • 8.) Drain; rinse with cold water and drain it again.
  • 9.)Using a large bowl, add the noodles, vegetables and steak; Finish off with the dressing
