Mac and Cheese Recipe.

Mac and Cheese is an easy to make a meal that with 1 small bite of the cheesy crunch you feel satisfied but you're still graving more.

.

"Kraft Mac and Cheese".

Ingredients:

  1. Wooden Spoon.
  2. Hot water/boul.
  3. Macaroni.
  4. Cheese sauce
  5. Milk.

How to make:

  1. Put water in a boul.
  2. Then put the Macaroni in.
  3. Wait untill the water is boiling and then take it out.
  4. Then add the cheese sauce to the macaroni.
  5. Mix.
  6. Then add milk to it.
  7. Mix.
  8. And there you have got your "Craft Mac and Cheese".
